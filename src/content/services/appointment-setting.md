## Overview

Most sales pipelines stall not because of bad leads, but because getting a qualified prospect into a confirmed, attended meeting is harder than it looks. Appointment setting sits at the exact junction between marketing interest and revenue — demanding a skill set different from both lead generation and closing: persistence without pressure, qualification judgment, clear verbal communication, and follow-through on reminder sequences that separate a booked slot from an actual conversation.

Teleforce provides inbound and outbound appointment-setting teams trained to your specific qualification criteria, working in your calendar and CRM in real time. Inbound agents handle overflow and after-hours inquiry calls, capturing and booking prospects who would otherwise reach voicemail or a contact form. Outbound agents work target lists with structured cadences — first contact, follow-up, and confirmation — applying your ICP filters before anything touches a sales rep's calendar. Both functions feed into confirmation and reminder workflows that are designed to drive show rates, not just bookings.

What good appointment setting looks like: every booked meeting has passed your qualification criteria, every attendee has received at least two pre-meeting touchpoints, every no-show triggers an immediate rebooking attempt, and your CRM reflects accurate disposition data your team can act on — discipline that is hard to sustain with an overextended in-house team or a generalist assistant.

## Who it's for

- **B2B companies with a defined sales motion but inconsistent top-of-funnel coverage.** If your account executives or field reps are spending meaningful time on cold outreach or chasing confirmations instead of running discovery calls, the function is misaligned. Separating appointment setting into a dedicated role restores capacity where it matters.
- **Service businesses with high-value, time-sensitive consultations.** Law firms, financial advisors, medical specialists, home services, and similar practices lose real revenue to no-shows. A team focused entirely on confirmation and reminder sequences, with immediate rebooking on cancellations, directly recovers that.
- **SaaS and technology companies launching outbound SDR programs.** Building an internal SDR bench is expensive and slow. An outsourced appointment-setting team can be deployed on a specific ICP and campaign sequence in weeks, generating qualified demo pipeline while your internal team scales.
- **Businesses expanding into U.S. Hispanic markets or Latin American accounts.** Reaching Spanish-speaking prospects with native-fluency agents — not accented or translated calls — meaningfully changes conversion at the top of funnel. Teleforce's bilingual teams handle EN and ES without switching teams or workflows.
- **Companies with high inbound volume but inadequate coverage across business hours.** If calls after 4 p.m. local or on Mondays are going unanswered, you are losing warm inbound at the worst possible moment. Extended-hours coverage without the overhead of a U.S.-based night team is a core use case for nearshore appointment setting.

## How it works

**Scoping and script development.** Before any agent makes or takes a call, we document your target profile, disqualification criteria, objection-handling parameters, and booking logistics. We draft call scripts and email/SMS templates and walk through them with your team. This is also when we identify which CRM fields matter, what calendar access looks like, and how escalations are handled when a prospect is ready to talk to a senior rep immediately.

**Integration and systems setup.** Agents are provisioned with access to your calendar, CRM, and any dialer or sequencing tools you use. Where Teleforce is running its own dialer infrastructure, we configure caller ID presentation, call recording, and disposition coding to match your reporting requirements. We test round-trip data flow — booked meeting in the calendar, record updated in CRM — before going live.

**Agent training and certification.** Agents complete a structured training program covering your product category, value proposition, common objections, and qualification criteria. They are not reading scripts verbatim; they are being trained to understand what they are selling well enough to handle deflection and re-engage a skeptical prospect. Training includes mock calls reviewed by a quality team before the agent is cleared for live volume.

**Soft launch and ramp.** The first one to two weeks run at reduced volume with tighter QA review — call recordings are sampled daily and calibration feedback is delivered quickly. This protects your brand during the learning curve and surfaces any script or criteria issues before they affect pipeline at scale.

**Ongoing QA, reporting, and optimization.** In steady state, agents receive regular call scoring and coaching. You receive reporting on contact rates, qualification rates, booking rates, show rates, and rebooking rates for no-shows. If a specific outreach sequence is underperforming — a particular time-of-day call block, a subject line, a call-back sequence — we surface it and propose an adjustment. The data is yours; we do not treat reporting as a black box.

## Why bilingual LATAM

Appointment setting is a voice-first function. The agent's communication style, fluency, and cultural register determine whether a prospect stays on the line or cuts the call short. Latin America produces a deep pool of university-educated professionals with accent-neutral English and native Spanish — a combination that is genuinely difficult to staff at scale in a U.S.-based operation. For companies reaching both English-dominant and Spanish-dominant buyers, Teleforce agents switch languages naturally within the same team and the same workflow, without routing calls to separate vendors or losing context.

Time-zone alignment is equally practical. LATAM nearshore teams work standard U.S. business hours — Eastern through Pacific — without the shift premiums tied to offshore operations running twelve hours apart. Agents are available when your prospects are most reachable and your sales managers are online for escalations. Agent retention in Teleforce's LATAM markets is structurally stronger than in high-churn domestic BPO environments — agents who stay long enough to internalize your product and buyer personas perform meaningfully better than those being continually replaced and retrained.
