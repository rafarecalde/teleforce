## Overview

Most B2B companies lose pipeline not at the close, but at the very top — too few qualified conversations entering the funnel each week. Building an internal SDR function to fix that is slow: a new sales development rep typically takes three to six months to ramp, carries a fully loaded annual cost well above six figures, and churns at high rates once trained. The math rarely works until a company has enough volume and process maturity to absorb that overhead.

Teleforce's Sales Lead Generation service inserts a production-ready SDR layer into your sales motion without the ramp delay or attrition risk. Our agents handle the full top-of-funnel sequence: researching and building targeted prospect lists, enriching your CRM with verified contact and firmographic data, executing personalized outbound sequences across phone and email, qualifying interest against your defined criteria, and delivering booked appointments directly onto your AE calendar. The output is a steady, predictable stream of qualified conversations — not raw leads that still require internal sorting.

Roughly 64% of companies now outsource at least part of their lead generation, according to recent industry benchmarks, making it one of the most commonly delegated B2B functions. The driver is simple: an outsourced program compresses time-to-first-meeting, removes the recruiting and management burden, and scales headcount up or down as pipeline targets shift — without adding permanent overhead. Done well, the engagement feels like an extension of your internal sales team, not a vendor operating independently.

## Who it's for

- **Early-stage B2B companies without a dedicated SDR function.** If your AEs are prospecting their own pipeline, selling time is being traded for admin work. A Teleforce SDR layer frees quota-carriers to focus on late-stage deals while the top of funnel stays active.
- **Mid-market companies entering a new vertical or geography.** Launching into a new segment requires unfamiliar list-build, different messaging, and outreach volume you cannot generate by stretching existing staff. An outsourced team can test the new market fast without a headcount commitment.
- **Companies with a Spanish-speaking buyer base they are not reaching effectively.** Whether the prospect is a U.S.-based Hispanic business owner or a procurement lead at a Latin American enterprise, English-only outreach consistently underperforms. Bilingual SDRs close that gap.
- **Seasonal or campaign-driven businesses.** If your pipeline cycles — trade-show follow-up surges, end-of-quarter blitzes, product launch windows — a flexible seat model lets you surge SDR capacity without hiring sprints you later have to unwind.
- **Sales teams with CRM debt.** If your existing database has aging contacts, incomplete firmographics, or unconverted MQLs that were never properly worked, enrichment and re-engagement sequences can unlock revenue already sitting in your system.

## How it works

**Scoping and ICP definition.** The engagement starts with a structured discovery session covering your ideal customer profile, deal size, sales cycle, objection patterns, and current CRM state. This session produces the qualification criteria agents use to score every prospect before it moves forward — not a generic checklist, but criteria calibrated to your specific deal motion.

**List build and CRM enrichment.** Agents construct targeted prospect lists from verified data sources matched against your ICP, then layer firmographic, technographic, and contact-level enrichment into your CRM before a single outreach touch is made. Lists are deduplicated against your existing pipeline to avoid prospect conflicts.

**Messaging alignment and approval.** Call scripts, email sequences, and voicemail frameworks are drafted by the Teleforce team, reviewed against your brand voice, and formally approved before go-live. Agents do not improvise on positioning — they execute approved talk-tracks and flag edge cases back for guidance.

**Outreach execution and qualification.** Once live, agents run multi-touch outbound sequences — cold call, email, follow-up call, nurture — following the cadence your sales process requires. Every prospect is qualified against your criteria before advancing; disqualified contacts are tagged and documented in your CRM with the reason, preserving data integrity for future campaigns.

**Warm handoff and appointment booking.** Qualified prospects are booked directly into AE calendars using your scheduling tool. At the time of handoff, the agent logs a structured call summary: what the prospect said, the confirmed need, any objections raised, and the next-step agreement. Your AE walks into the meeting with context, not a blank slate.

**QA and performance reporting.** Teleforce supervisors conduct call reviews, messaging audits, and conversion-rate analysis on an ongoing cadence. You receive regular performance reports covering dials, contacts reached, qualified leads, meetings set, and show rate — the metrics that matter for forecasting pipeline, not vanity activity counts.

## Why bilingual LATAM

Outbound success in bilingual markets is not just about language — it is about register, cadence, and cultural fit. A prospect who speaks Spanish at home and English in business can tell within the first thirty seconds whether the person calling them code-switches naturally or is reading from a translated script. Teleforce SDRs are native bilingual speakers trained in U.S. business norms, which means they navigate both sides of that conversation without seams: English for the gatekeeper, Spanish for the owner, back to English for the follow-up email — without a hand-off, without delay.

Nearshore Latin America also solves the time-zone problem that offshore lead generation consistently fails on. Your SDRs are working the same business hours as your prospects and your AEs — morning standups, real-time Slack, same-day escalation. Turnover rates in LATAM BPO markets are structurally lower than in domestic SDR roles, which means the agent who learns your product and your ICP in week two is still on the account in month eight. That continuity compounds: messaging improves, objection handling sharpens, and conversion rates trend up rather than resetting every time a seat turns over.
