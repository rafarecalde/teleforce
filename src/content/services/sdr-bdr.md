## Overview

Pipeline problems usually aren't closing problems — they're volume problems at the top. Not enough qualified conversations enter the funnel each week, so quota-carriers spend their time prospecting instead of selling. Building an in-house SDR bench to fix that is slow and expensive: a new sales development rep takes three to six months to ramp, carries a fully loaded cost well into six figures, and churns fast once trained.

A Teleforce **SDR / BDR team** inserts a production-ready outbound layer into your motion without the ramp delay or attrition risk. Dedicated, bilingual nearshore reps own the full top-of-funnel sequence — researching and building targeted lists, running personalized multi-touch outreach across call, email, and LinkedIn, qualifying interest against your criteria, and booking confirmed, sales-ready meetings straight onto your closers' calendars. Your AEs get a steady stream of qualified conversations; you get an outbound engine that scales with your targets, not your headcount plan.

## Who it's for

- **Founders and AEs prospecting their own pipeline.** If quota-carriers are building their own top of funnel, selling time is being spent on admin. A dedicated SDR layer frees them to work late-stage deals.
- **Teams entering a new segment or geography.** New markets need fresh lists, new messaging, and outreach volume you can't generate by stretching existing staff — without a permanent headcount commitment.
- **Companies with a Spanish-speaking buyer base they aren't reaching.** Bilingual reps open the U.S. Hispanic market and Latin American accounts that English-only outreach consistently underperforms on.
- **Pipeline that cycles.** Trade-show follow-up surges, end-of-quarter blitzes, launch windows — a flexible seat model lets you surge outbound capacity and return to baseline without hiring sprints.

## How it works

**Scope and ICP.** We map your ideal customer profile, deal motion, objection patterns, and CRM state, and set the qualification bar every prospect is measured against before it advances.

**Ramp and approval.** Reps train on your product and talk-track; call scripts, email sequences, and voicemail frameworks are drafted, reviewed against your brand voice, and approved before go-live.

**Outbound and qualification.** Reps run multi-touch sequences at the cadence your process requires, qualify each prospect against your criteria, and log every touch and disposition in your CRM — disqualified contacts tagged with a reason so your data stays clean.

**Booked meetings.** Qualified prospects are handed off as confirmed meetings on your closers' calendars, with a discovery note attached — no cold calendar invites, no AE prep from scratch.
