## Overview

When users hit a technical wall — an integration that won't connect, a feature that behaves differently than documented, a device that stopped syncing after an update — how fast and how clearly the problem gets resolved determines whether they stay or churn. Teleforce delivers bilingual Tier 1 and Tier 2 technical support for software and hardware companies that need a capable, always-on support team without the cost and lead time of building one in-house.

Tier 1 is your first line: agents who know your product well enough to resolve the highest-volume, repeatable issues on first contact — guided setup, common error troubleshooting, account management, and knowledge base look-ups — without escalating unnecessarily. Tier 2 sits directly behind it, handling cases that require log review, environment-specific diagnosis, escalation coordination with your engineering or product team, or hands-on configuration work. Both tiers operate under the same team structure and quality controls, so handoffs between them are clean and documented, not a gap where tickets fall silent.

What good looks like in tech support is specific: first-contact resolution above baseline for your product category, average handle time that reflects actual complexity rather than rushed closures, escalation rates that trend downward as agents learn, and customers who feel heard in the language they reached out in. Teleforce tracks all of these and reports them to you on a cadence you set.

## Who it's for

- **SaaS companies scaling past their founders' inbox.** Early-stage teams often handle support ad hoc until volume makes that untenable. If your engineering team is closing tickets, tech support outsourcing frees them for product work without sacrificing response quality.
- **Hardware and IoT brands with complex setup flows.** Physical products — smart home devices, connected equipment, peripherals — generate disproportionate support contact at unboxing and firmware update moments. Agents trained on your specific device line handle these peaks without requiring a permanent headcount spike.
- **Software vendors serving bilingual or Spanish-dominant markets.** If a meaningful share of your users prefer Spanish — whether U.S. Hispanic markets or Latin American customers — routing them to English-only support creates friction and CSAT drag. Teleforce resolves contacts natively in both languages.
- **Product teams launching new features or migrating platforms.** Launch windows and migrations create a predictable surge in Tier 1 contacts. Teleforce can staff for that surge without the long-term commitment of a new hire class, and then scale back when volume normalizes.
- **Companies with after-hours or weekend coverage gaps.** Many U.S. tech teams operate core business hours. Teleforce operates during U.S. business hours by default and can extend coverage windows — useful for products with consumer audiences that generate support volume evenings and weekends.

## How it works

**Scoping and documentation review.** The engagement starts with a structured discovery session: your product, your current ticket categories and volumes, your existing KB (or lack of one), your escalation matrix, and the tools your team uses. This shapes the training plan and staffing profile before anyone is assigned.

**Agent selection and product training.** Teleforce selects agents whose technical background and language profile match your product category. Training is hands-on and product-specific — agents work through your onboarding flow, stress-test common failure paths, and complete a knowledge check before handling live contacts. For Managed BPO engagements, a team lead coordinates the training process and owns the ongoing KB.

**Tool integration and access provisioning.** Agents are provisioned into your ticketing platform, CRM, or any internal tool required to do the job. We work within your security policies — SSO, role-based permissions, audit logging. No parallel infrastructure to manage on your side.

**Supervised soft launch.** The first one to two weeks of live contacts run with elevated QA — a higher percentage of interactions reviewed, daily calibration calls with your team, and rapid adjustments to scripts, KB articles, or escalation rules based on what real contacts reveal. This is the highest-ROI window for catching misalignments early.

**Ongoing QA, reporting, and calibration.** After go-live, Teleforce delivers reporting on the metrics that matter for tech support: FCR rate, average handle time, ticket aging, escalation rate, CSAT (where survey tools are in place), and KB utilization. Monthly or quarterly business reviews give your team a structured opportunity to realign priorities, adjust staffing, or expand coverage.
